One ring a month.

Every month gets a ring. Purchases fill it in meals for your cause, and the gift closes it. The dollars are always exact, one line underneath.

  1. I

    Shop like normal.

    Each purchase that reaches Givv counts its change to the next dollar, plus a flat Round Up+ of $1, $2, $5 or $10. A $4.35 coffee sets aside $1.65. Apple Pay taps arrive through a Shortcuts automation you set up.

  2. II

    Watch the ring fill.

    About 33¢ a meal at Riverside, a sample charity. The tally shows as meals for your charity, up to a monthly cap you choose. Follow up to three causes.

  3. III

    Give on Give day.

    Givv opens the charity’s own donation page. You give there, then confirm in Givv. You have a week.

  4. IV

    Open your month.

    It arrives sealed. Pull the strip, the ring closes, and your streak counts one more.

Twelve rings make your year.

Each closed month keeps its meals, and the total since you joined never resets. And the streak comes with a safety net.

A week of grace

Give day is the 1st, the 15th or the last day of the month. Your month counts until 11:59pm seven days later.

Take a month off

Pause any month before its Give day. It isn’t counted, isn’t missed, and gets no reminders.

Give Day Shield

One per quarter. If a week of grace ends without a gift, the Shield holds your streak automatically.

Givv never touches your money.

It counts, reminds and celebrates. The giving happens between you and your charity.

Your gift goes straight to the charity

You give on the charity’s own donation page, and Givv takes nothing. They send your tax receipt.

No card numbers, no bank logins

For each purchase, Givv keeps the store name, amount and date, encrypted in its ledger so your tally is right on every device. It can’t move money.

No cut of your gifts

Givv is a membership through Apple: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, first month free. None of it comes out of what you give.

You set the limits

Set a monthly cap, choose which kinds of purchase count, leave out any single purchase, or change the amount on Give day.

Fair questions.

Does Givv take money from my account?

No. Givv only counts. On Give day it opens your charity’s donation page and you decide there: the full amount, a different amount, or not yet.

What does Givv see?

The store name, amount and date of each purchase, stored encrypted so your tally is right on every device. Givv never sees card numbers or bank logins and can’t move money.

What if I miss a month?

You have a week after Give day. If that week ends without a gift, your quarter’s Give Day Shield holds the streak. If the Shield is already used, the streak starts again with the next ring, and everything you’ve given stays in your year.

What does the membership pay for?

The round-up tracking, your monthly ring and the Give day reminders. It’s $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, billed through Apple after a free first month, and none of it comes out of your gifts. Giving on your charity’s page, your history and your giving record never need a membership.

Can I control how much I give?

Yes. Pick the Round Up+ extra ($1, $2, $5 or $10), choose which purchases count, set a monthly cap and leave out any purchase. You also decide the final amount on Give day.

Who sends my tax receipt?

Your charity, because you give on their own donation page. Givv keeps a yearly history you can export for your records.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Givv is iPhone only for now.
